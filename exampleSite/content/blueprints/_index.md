Every blueprint gives you the front matter and the markdown body for
one kind of page, with notes on what to replace. They are recipes, not
live demos: the rendered result of a blueprint is what your own page
will look like, not what this page looks like.

Filters combine two axes. The first is the section a blueprint builds
on (`landing`, `docs`, `articles`), the second is the context it suits
(`oss`, `commercial`).

For the full parameter tables behind every shortcode used here, see the
[shortcode reference](/docs/shortcodes).
